# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, GSAP, Lenis, Three.js / React Three Fiber

## Users
Recruiters, engineering leads, lecturers, and fellow developers evaluating Dhimas's portfolio and technical work in a single visit.

## Product Purpose
Introduce Dhimas through a high-craft single-page portfolio that proves engineering rigor, aesthetic restraint, and creative front-end interaction.

## Positioning
A calm, content-first portfolio anchored by an interactive physics-driven 3D lanyard hero, delivering memorable tactile interaction without sacrificing readability or performance.

## Operating Context
Evaluated on desktop and mobile web browsers. Requires fast loading, fluid transitions, bilingual support (ID/EN), and seamless light/dark mode adaptation.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Single-page architecture with five sections: Home, About, Experience, Projects, Contacts
- Interactive 3D lanyard simulation with WebGL and non-WebGL fallback
- Synchronized motion: Lenis smooth scroll, GSAP ScrollTrigger, and Framer Motion layout transitions
- Light (cool off-white `#F4F7FC`) and dark (charcoal `#1F2227`) themes
- Bilingual toggle (Bahasa Indonesia default, English option)
- Accessibility compliance: respects `prefers-reduced-motion` and semantic HTML standards

## Brand Commitments
- Identity: Dhimas
- Voice & feel: Clean, alive, light, calm ("bersih, hidup, ringan, tenang")
- Anti-slop constraints: Avoid templated cards, artificial arrow buttons, or unmotivated decoration
